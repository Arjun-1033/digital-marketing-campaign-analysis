## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze digital marketing campaign performance.

The dashboard provides insights into campaign investment, impressions, platform and advertisement performance, marketing funnel conversion, and audience targeting.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall digital marketing campaign performance
- Track campaign budgets and impressions
- Compare platform and advertisement performance
- Analyze the marketing funnel from impressions to purchases
- Identify audience segments and targeting patterns
- Evaluate campaign engagement and conversion metrics

---

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of campaign performance using key KPIs such as:

- Total Budget
- Total Campaigns
- Total Impressions
- Cost Per Purchase
- Conversion Rate

It also includes campaign budget distribution, event type distribution, and trends over time.

### 2. Platform & Ad Performance
Compares marketing performance across platforms and advertisement types using:

- Click Through Rate (CTR)
- Engagement Rate
- Purchase Rate
- Campaign Duration
- Budget vs Events
- Ad Type Performance

### 3. Funnel Analysis
Analyzes the customer journey from impressions to purchases.

The page focuses on:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- Engagement
- Purchases
- Drop-off Rate
- Purchase Rate
- Conversion Rate

### 4. Audience & Targeting Fit
Analyzes audience characteristics and purchasing behavior based on:

- Age Group
- Gender
- Country
- Interests
- Engagement

---

## 🔑 Key Insights

- The campaigns generated approximately **340K impressions**.
- The total campaign budget analyzed was approximately **2.54M**.
- The dataset contains **50 campaigns**.
- A significant drop-off occurs as users move through the marketing funnel from impressions to purchases.
- Platform and advertisement performance varies across CTR, engagement, and purchase rates.
- Purchase behavior differs across age groups and genders.
- Audience and geographic analysis can help identify patterns in campaign engagement and purchases.
